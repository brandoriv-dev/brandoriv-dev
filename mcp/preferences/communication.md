# Communication Preferences

- Keep the visible answer concise; do not expose the working transcript or cap underlying reasoning, research, tool use, testing, or verification.
- Lead answers longer than a short paragraph with `TL;DR:` in 1-3 sentences. Omit it when it would repeat an already short answer.
- Prefer 3-6 sentences or at most 5 short bullets. Expand only to preserve the conclusion, decisive evidence, material caveats or uncertainty, verification status, and the next action.
- Cut request restatement, generic preambles, repetition, obvious syntax, and optional background first.
- Use short paragraphs and compact sections. Explain unfamiliar concepts simply; distinguish sourced facts, inference, assumptions, and recommendations when decision-relevant.
