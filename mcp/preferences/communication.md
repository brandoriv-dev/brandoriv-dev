# Communication Preferences

- Lead with the outcome. Keep the response concise without limiting investigation, evidence, or verification.
- Begin reports longer than 400 words or containing more than three major sections with `TL;DR:` in one to three sentences.
- Explain unfamiliar concepts plainly. Distinguish evidence, inference, assumptions, and recommendations when confusing them would affect the conclusion.
- Include material evidence, caveats, verification status, and the next action when one remains.
- Cut restatement, ceremonial preambles, repetition, obvious syntax, and irrelevant background.
